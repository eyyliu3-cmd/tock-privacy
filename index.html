Privacy Policy

Tock — hourly chime for Wear OS · Last updated 18 October 2026

Tock’s own code does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What Tock collects

Nothing. Tock has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no user accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

Your settings

Your chime settings — active hours, days, alert type, intensity, offset, quiet windows, and chime sound — are stored only on your watch. They are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling Tock removes them.

Network access

Tock’s own code makes no network connections, and your settings never leave your watch. Tock includes Google’s Play Billing library so you can buy Tock Pro. That library connects to Google Play to check purchases, and sends Google basic diagnostic information about how it is working, such as connection errors. Tock does not see or control that data; Google’s privacy policy covers it. Everything else in Tock works offline.

Permissions Tock uses

  • Exact alarms — to schedule the chime at the exact top of the hour. This is the app's core function.
  • Notifications — to show the hourly chime. You may decline this; Tock remains usable.
  • Vibration — to produce the chime's vibration.

None of these permissions transmit data off your watch.

Purchases

Tock Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. Tock never sees, collects, or stores payment information. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Tock is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
